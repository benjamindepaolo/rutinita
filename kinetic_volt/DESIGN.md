## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for elite athletic output, structured workout logging, and high-velocity biometric tracking under demanding physical conditions. 

- **Personality:** Kinetic, precise, uncompromising, and electric. It channels the focus of athletic high-performance environments—sweat, iron, precision chronometers, and relentless velocity.
- **Target Audience:** Dedicated lifters, endurance athletes, track athletes, and data-driven fitness practitioners who require zero-friction logging during maximal exertion.
- **Emotional Response:** Sharp alertness, tunnel-vision focus, raw empowerment, and unwavering confidence in collected metrics.
- **Design Movement:** High-Contrast Performance Minimalist fused with Tactical Digital Readouts. True-black OLED backgrounds maximize battery life and eliminate visual noise, while hyper-saturated electric lime cuts through bright gym lighting, sweat-induced glare, and quick glances between heavy sets.

## Colors

The palette leverages pure-black zero-emission OLED surfaces layered with deep slate structural containers, electrified by high-wavelength phosphorescent accents.

- **Primary (`#C6FF00` - Electric Lime):** Used exclusively for high-intent focal points: active sets, completion CTAs, live chronometers, current PR badges, and peak velocity graphs. Ensures instantaneous legibility at arm's length.
- **Secondary (`#0F172A` - Slate 900):** Provides architectural zoning. Elevated cards, module backgrounds, and segmented controls anchor on slate variants (`#1E293B` to `#0F172A`) to prevent visual fatigue while preserving deep value separation.
- **Tertiary (`#FF334B` - Crimson Impact):** Communicates hard limits, maximal heart rate zones, RPE 10 warnings, failed reps, and critical countdown triggers.
- **Neutral (`#0B0E14` - Pitch Slate) & Surface Hierarchy:** Pure black (`#000000`) canvas ground with graphite tiers (`#131822`, `#1A2231`, `#242F42`) establishing clear spatial order. High-contrast typography transitions from optical white (`#F8FAFC`) to muted technical steel (`#64748B`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability and structural precision under high cognitive strain and physical exhaustion.

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Industrial, wide-aperture sans-serif providing authoritative hierarchy. Tight letter-spacing gives workout routines, exercise names, and phase titles a mechanical, urgent quality.
- **Body & Controls (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Highly legible, human-engineered grotesque offering clean clarity for instructions, rep breakdowns, and notes without competing with primary data points.
- **Biometric Readouts & Labels (`Space Mono`):** Fixed-width tabular figures guarantee numbers never jitter during rapid timer ticks, live barbell velocity inputs, or heart rate fluctuations. Capitalized tracking brings a tactical telemetry feel to labels and units.

## Layout & Spacing

Workout environments demand generous ergonomics to account for shaky hands, chalked fingers, and brief glance windows.

- **Grid Model:** An 8pt baseline rhythm adapted to a flexible 4-column layout on mobile devices, expanding to 8 columns on tablet and 12 columns on desktop workout consoles.
- **Touch Boundaries:** Critical interactive logging triggers (rep tick counters, set completions, timer modifiers) strictly mandate a minimum bounding box of 56px height, far exceeding standard mobile minimums.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation:**
  - *Mobile (< 640px):* Single-column vertical stream. Fixed sticky bottom telemetry deck for rapid set logging. Margins clamped to `1rem` to maximize actionable screen real estate.
  - *Tablet (640px - 1024px):* Split layout balancing workout tracking sequence on the left 60% and rest timer/volume curves on the right 40%.
  - *Desktop (> 1024px):* Fixed analytical dashboard framing macro progression graphs against detailed daily meso-cycle logs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on soft, ambient drop shadows, which wash out on pure black backgrounds and diminish contrast. Instead, depth is achieved via tonal surface stratification and hyper-focused luminous highlights.

- **Tonal Layers:** 
  - Base Floor: `#000000` (Pure Black canvas).
  - Level 1 Surface: `#0B0E14` (Module groupings, split screen sections).
  - Level 2 Container: `#131822` (Data cards, inactive set rows).
  - Level 3 Active Target: `#1A2231` (Currently selected input, active rest drawer).
- **Edge Definition:** Thin structural lines (1px) rendered in `#242F42` provide crisp delineation between cards without cluttering the screen.
- **Luminous Edge & Focus Rings:** Active input states discard blurs in favor of a 1.5px solid boundary in `#C6FF00` accompanied by a localized phosphor bloom (`0 0 16px rgba(198, 255, 0, 0.25)`).
- **Overlays & Modals:** Deep semi-transparent backdrop sheets (`#000000` at 85% opacity) with a subtle `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` allow underlying telemetry to provide positional awareness while foreground numbers command total focus.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a low-radius, athletic-technical geometry (Level 1: Soft). 

- **Surfaces & Cards:** Defined by a compact 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius. This conveys architectural solidity and computational precision, avoiding the softness of consumer apps.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Primary actions use 6px (`0.375rem`) to 8px (`0.5rem` / `rounded-lg`) maximum radius. Complete pill-shapes are restricted solely to live status chips (e.g., "LIVE RECORDING", "HEART RATE LOCK") to preserve their semantic distinction from actionable controls.
- **Data Cells & Inputs:** Strict 4px corners keep multi-column weight and repetition grids crisp, aligned, and dense with no wasted perimeter spacing.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary CTA (Log Set / Finish Workout):** High-voltage `#C6FF00` background with deep slate black typography (`#000000`) set in `Space Grotesk Bold`. Minimum height of 58px on mobile with tactile haptic response.
- **Secondary Actions (Add Exercise / Substitute):** Dark slate surface (`#131822`) with 1px border (`#242F42`) and white text (`#F8FAFC`). Hover/Active state turns border to `#C6FF00`.
- **Destructive / Abort Actions:** Transparent background with `#FF334B` border and crimson text, filling with solid crimson only upon explicit press-and-hold confirmation.

### Data Cards & Logging Grids
- **Workout Set Row:** Segmented layout containing Set Number (`Space Mono`), Previous Weight/Reps preview (`#64748B`), Target Input, and Checkmark Trigger. 
- **Completed Set State:** Subtle `#C6FF00` 1px left-border accent with dim `#131822` fill, locking the inputs and advancing cursor focus automatically to the next sequential row.
- **Metric Widgets:** Dark slate cards displaying live load, volume progression, and velocity loss. Numerals rendered in oversized `metric-numeral` scale.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** High-contrast pill containers featuring a glowing 6px pulsating dot. Live sets feature an electric lime beacon; warning thresholds utilize crimson red.
- **RPE / Intensity Chips:** Small rectangular tags with 2px corner radius using monospace alphanumeric ratings (`RPE 8.5`, `1RM 92%`).

### Form Inputs & Steppers
- **Rep & Weight Steppers:** Symmetrical dual-button pods flanking a large central editable number display. Plus/minus buttons utilize heavy 48px hit areas to prevent mis-clicks with sweaty hands.
- **Direct Entry Keypad:** Custom in-app bottom sheet numerical pad featuring tabular numbers with high-contrast tactile divisions, bypassing the OS keyboard for speed.

### Dynamic Data Visualization
- **Barbell Velocity & Zone Charts:** Micro-sparklines and vertical frequency bars rendered using luminous gradient strokes descending from `#C6FF00` to `#0F172A`.
- **Rest Interval Progress Ring:** High-visibility ring stroke utilizing `#C6FF00` on a dark `#1A2231` track, dynamically shifting to flashing `#FF334B` during the final 5-second countdown.
